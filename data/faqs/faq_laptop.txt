Laptop FAQ

Python programming support: The catalog laptops listed with an Intel Core i5, AMD Ryzen 5/7, or Apple M1 processor can run Python 3 and common editors such as VS Code. 16 GB RAM is recommended for comfortable multitasking. These products are consumer laptops, not dedicated servers.

Machine learning compatibility: Introductory Python notebooks, scikit-learn, and small CPU-based machine-learning exercises can run on the listed laptops. Training large deep-learning models locally is not guaranteed; use a cloud GPU for larger workloads. Dedicated graphics are only present when explicitly listed in the product specification.

Battery details: Battery-life figures in the catalog are manufacturer or illustrative estimates. Actual runtime varies with screen brightness, workload, wireless use, and battery age. CPU-intensive coding and model training reduce runtime.

Warranty information: Warranty terms vary by brand, seller, and region. Confirm the current warranty and return policy with the retailer before purchase. Keep the invoice and serial number for service requests.

Online classes: All listed laptops support browser-based video meetings when connected to the internet. Camera quality and microphone specifications should be confirmed on the manufacturer's product page.
